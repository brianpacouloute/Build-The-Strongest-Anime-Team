USAGE NOTES
- Repeat the cream and dark tiles at their natural aspect ratio. Stretching a tile stretches its dots.
- Card backgrounds cover the item art area. Add your own text and lower information panel.
- Overlay corner frames separately so that their opacity can be adjusted.
- Dashed borders are supplied in a wide tab shape. Large aspect-ratio changes distort dash lengths.
- For flexible corner frames, split into four 64 × 64px source regions at the four image corners and anchor them independently.
- Add dark square backgrounds behind the check/cross to reproduce the ownership badges.
- The pale border version is for active navigation tabs; tan is for inactive tabs.
- UI labels, item renders, layout code, and unrelated icons are not included.
